A Thanksgiving Reflection

Gratitude Beyond Limits

When I think of something to be grateful for, like many others I gravitate towards the positive things in my life. And its easy to do so. Much of our worlds are built based on societal views and what’s leading the entertainment industry. It takes a special kind of individual to know and understand that gratitude shouldn’t be expressed only for the positive things in our lives. In some ways, it seems a little transactional when we can only be thankful for the good and leave behind the feeling of overall gratitude without expectation.

Though we have been normalized to the idea that gratitude should only exist when things are positive, it leaves room to wonder where these ideas have stemmed. In many cases, these ideas are passed down through familial beliefs, societal conditioning, and other practices. Some even feel they have nothing to be grateful for if they are not receiving positive things in their life.

What if the search for something better is not all that is to be grateful for? Once we move towards a life of overall gratitude even outside of the positives in life we begin to experience life as a whole instead of just in parts. Seeking out the positives in life leaves a lot of life by the way side. In a lifetime, there are only so many great things. But there are usually a multitude of mundane things, normal everyday things, and even “bad” things. All of which are subjective.

When you’re grateful only for the good parts in life, then you leave much on the table. A lot of life is lived in the mundane moments that we experience. Can we not also share gratitude for those moments? The everyday moments of life are what brings us to the highlighted moments in the journey. This is not to say that you’re being directed to be thankful for difficult times in your life. Far from it. However, the parts of you that you find in the midst of navigating those difficult times is something to be grateful for.

This season and for those moving forward, you’re being invited to live a life of gratitude without partiality towards the positive. Things will happen. Things, experiences, events, these are all parts of a full life. When we move away from expectation and move into fulfillment, it will prove to be a more graceful life. Even without asking. Think about one way that you can be more grateful for the mundane everyday moments and try to incorporate that gratitude in all you do.

Thanks for reading!
